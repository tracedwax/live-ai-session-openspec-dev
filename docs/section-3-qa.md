# Section 3: Reconvene

About 15 minutes, everyone together. Bring both rooms back, see that the same flow produced a buildable design and tasks, and clear up whatever is still fuzzy.

## Show two results

One per room, about 90 seconds each, screen shared. Show what the spec became, the design and the tasks. Say where you split a spec and what told you it was too big. Say whether you would build from it, and whether every task traces to a decision.

## Questions

Common ones. How big should a spec be: one behavior a user can observe, and if it needs more than six to eight WHEN/THEN scenarios, split it. Where does product stop and the developer start: that split is out of scope today and a team decision; today you ran the whole thing so you can trust a spec you are handed. Full-stack or split by layer: full-stack by behavior, never frontend, backend, or by area of the page. What if the spec is wrong: tell whoever owns it; the spec is a decision record, fixed before you build, not patched after.

If the room is quiet, ask what almost stopped them. The friction is the most useful thing for next time.

## Close

You turned a spec into something you could build, one behavior at a time. Next time we connect the front and back halves end to end. Go to the closing.
